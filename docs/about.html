About Me

The longer version.

I'm Gaurang — pronounced Gaw-rung. I spent seven years building production software, and somewhere along the way I got genuinely curious about what happens when engineering meets machine learning. So I decided to find out properly, which is how I ended up at UBC doing a Master of Data Science.

My engineering background is in Java microservices, CI/CD, and keeping systems running under pressure. At UKG I grew from developer to lead, owning core services, modernizing infrastructure, and mentoring junior engineers along the way. Before that, Ericsson and Apple taught me what it means to care about correctness — I converted MATLAB prototypes to production C++ at Ericsson, and cut Wi-Fi test runtimes by over 70% at Apple.

What MDS has added is the ability to think statistically and work across the full ML stack — from wrangling messy data and training models to building reproducible pipelines and deploying interactive tools. I care about models that work in production, not just ones that look good in a notebook.

Right now I'm working on a capstone project with Educational Testing Service (ETS), building an equity-focused education dashboard that processes over 1.7 million student records across 80+ countries. It's the kind of work where the technical decisions actually matter for how policymakers understand global education outcomes.

I'm open to ML/AI engineering and data science roles — hybrid or remote. If you're working on something interesting, let's talk.
